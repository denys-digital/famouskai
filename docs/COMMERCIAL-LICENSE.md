2. **Commercial License** — for organizations that want to integrate Famouskai (or Diffskai) into a
   proprietary product without triggering the AGPLv3 copyleft/source-disclosure obligations.

## Why a commercial license might be required

The AGPLv3 is fine for personal use, academic use, internal tools you don't distribute, and open-source
projects that accept the same license. It is generally **not** compatible with:

- Closed-source SaaS products built on top of Famouskai/Diffskai.
- Proprietary desktop or content management system (CMS) integrations you intend to keep closed.
- Any redistribution where you do not want to release your own modifications' source code.

If your use case falls into one of these categories, you need a commercial license.

## What a commercial license includes

- The right to use, modify, and embed Famouskai/Diffskai in closed-source products.
- No obligation to disclose your modifications or your product's source code.
- (Optional, negotiable) priority support / a service-level agreement (SLA) — to be defined case
  by case.

## How to acquire one

Contact the author directly via [github.com/denys-digital](https://github.com/denys-digital) with:

- A short description of your intended use (product, scale, deployment context).
- Whether you need ongoing updates or a one-time integration license.

Pricing is tailored to the scope of use (indie/startup vs. enterprise). There is no self-serve
checkout at this stage — every commercial license is discussed directly with the maintainer.

## Contributor code and the commercial license

Because Famouskai accepts external contributions, every contributor signs the
[Contributor License Agreement](./CLA.md) before their first pull request is merged. This is what
legally allows the maintainer to keep offering a commercial license that includes community
contributions — without it, the maintainer could not sublicense contributed code outside of AGPLv3,
and the dual-license model would break down over time. See [`CLA.md`](./CLA.md) for details.
