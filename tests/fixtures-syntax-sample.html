Famous Mark tester

A Famous Mark is one of three characters at the start of a line: «﹥», «•» or «°». Famouskai lists those lines in the Outline tab and folds the text down to the next mark. In any other editor they are just characters — no lock-in.

The three marks

MarkNameCode pointSnippet
﹥Famous ArrowU+FE65/fa + Tab
•Magic DotU+2022/md + Tab
°Flying BubbleU+00B0/fb + Tab
