# Famouskai DNA

**Fast. Private. Resilient.**
Your files, on your disk, in your browser. Nothing in between.

**Famouskai opens in a second, works offline, and never loses your work.**

## 100%

- **100% local.** Famouskai opens the file on your disk and saves it in place, online or offline.
- **100% safe for your work.** Nothing changes silently: every change, by you or by a tool, shows as unsaved until you save it. If another program changed the file, Diffskai lets you choose what to keep.
- **100% desktop.** Built for a keyboard, a mouse and a large screen, in a desktop Chromium browser.
- **100% open.** AGPLv3, for good. A commercial license exists for those who cannot share their code.
- **100% extendable.** Colors are plain CSS, tools are small JavaScript files: anyone can read one and write one in an afternoon.

## Zero

- **Zero account.** Open Famouskai and work. Nobody should have to sign in to edit a text file.
- **Zero setup.** It runs in the browser and installs as an app in one click, even on a Chromebook without Linux: no command line, no virtual machine, no developer mode.
- **Zero server.** Your files never leave your machine; only your settings and history stay, locally in your browser.
- **Zero tracker.** No analytics, no ads, no telemetry.
- **Zero lock-in.** Your files stay plain text. Famous Marks are ordinary characters that any editor can read.
- **Zero overhead.** One document per page: your browser already has tabs.
- **Zero CDN.** Every library ships in the repository: nothing loads from a third party.
- **Zero build.** No bundler, no npm install: the code you read is the code that runs.

*If a change breaks one of these lines, it does not belong in Famouskai, however useful it is.*
